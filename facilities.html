Corex Facilities

Explore the equipment and dedicated training areas available at Corex gyms.

Our Facilities

Corex provides a range of facilities designed to support different types of training. Whether you are interested in cardiovascular exercise, strength training or functional fitness, our gyms provide dedicated spaces and equipment for your workout.

Our facilities are maintained regularly to provide members with a clean, safe and comfortable environment for training.

Cardio Area

The Corex cardio area provides equipment for improving cardiovascular fitness and endurance.

  • Treadmills
  • Exercise Bikes
  • Cross Trainers
  • Rowing Machines

Weight Training

Our weight training area provides equipment for members looking to develop strength, muscle and overall fitness.

  • Free Weights
  • Dumbbells
  • Barbells
  • Weight Machines

Functional Training

The functional training area is designed for exercises that focus on movement, balance, coordination and overall physical performance.

  • Kettlebells
  • Resistance Bands
  • Medicine Balls
  • Training Ropes

Stretching Area

A dedicated area where members can warm up, cool down and perform stretching exercises before or after their workouts.

  • Exercise Mats
  • Foam Rollers
  • Stretching Equipment
  • Open Floor Space

Equipment & Safety

Corex equipment is arranged to provide members with sufficient space to train comfortably. Equipment is regularly checked and maintained to help provide a safe training environment.

Members are encouraged to use equipment responsibly and follow the instructions provided for each machine. Staff are available to assist members who require guidance.
